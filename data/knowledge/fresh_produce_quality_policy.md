# Fresh Produce Quality Policy

Source ID: FM-POL-001
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: FreshMart Australia (fictional)

This document is a fictional demonstration policy, not the policy of any real
Australian retailer. It is not legal advice.

## Quality concerns

Customers may report mould, unexpected spoilage, bruising, or wilting in fresh
produce purchased from a fictional FreshMart store. Strawberries showing mould
are a quality concern that should be referred for review. Customers should not
eat produce they believe is unsafe.

## Information for review

Record the store, product, purchase date, and a description of the issue.
Customers may indicate whether proof of purchase is available. Do not request
names, contact information, receipt images, payment details, or other personal
information in this demonstration.

## Relationship to refund requests

Refer to the Refund and Exchange Policy (FM-POL-002) for the fictional review
conditions. A quality concern or a submitted complaint does not establish refund
approval. An authorised team member must review the circumstances.

## Internal quality monitoring

Store teams use recorded inspection weights to monitor freshness:
Freshness Pass Rate = total PassedKg / total InspectedKg.
Sales volume is not a measure of freshness. Complaint and inspection patterns
can support an investigation but do not prove a cause.
