# Online Order Missing Items Policy

Source ID: FM-POL-003
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: FreshMart Australia (fictional)

This document is a fictional demonstration policy, not the policy of any real
Australian retailer.

## Missing item review

For the fictional service, report missing online order items within 48 hours
of delivery or collection. In a real service, an authorised team member would
check the order contents, substitutions, and fulfilment record before deciding
an outcome.

## Demonstration limitations

This prototype cannot access online orders, verify deliveries, or create a
missing-item refund. Explain the policy and direct the customer to an authorised
team member. Do not request real order identifiers or personal information.
The prototype complaint form is for fresh produce quality concerns only.

## Outcomes

A missing-item report is not automatic refund approval. Do not invent delivery
records, compensation amounts, or payment timelines.
