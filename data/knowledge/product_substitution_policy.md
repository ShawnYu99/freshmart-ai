# Product Substitution Policy

Source ID: FM-POL-004
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: FreshMart Australia (fictional)

This document is a fictional demonstration policy, not the policy of any real
Australian retailer.

## Substitution review

In this fictional service, an unavailable online product may be substituted only
where the customer enabled substitutions for that order. Staff should consider
the requested product type and declared order preferences. Do not guarantee
that a substitute meets a customer's allergy or dietary requirements.

Customers concerned about a substituted item can ask an authorised team member
to review the order. The reviewer checks the customer's substitution preference
and what was supplied before deciding a remedy.

## Demonstration limitations

The assistant cannot inspect order preferences, change orders, make medical
recommendations, or approve refunds. No specific substitution price adjustment
or refund entitlement is defined in these demonstration documents. Acknowledge
that missing information instead of inventing a rule.
