# Refund and Exchange Policy

Source ID: FM-POL-002
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: FreshMart Australia (fictional)

This document is a fictional demonstration policy, not the policy of any real
Australian retailer. It is not legal advice or a statement of Australian
consumer law. The demonstration conditions do not describe or limit statutory
consumer rights.

## Fresh produce quality review

For this fictional workflow, customers should report a fresh produce quality
concern within seven calendar days of purchase. Provide the FreshMart store,
product, purchase date, and issue description. The reviewer considers whether
the issue was present at purchase or became apparent shortly afterwards and
whether the produce was stored appropriately.

Proof of purchase helps the review. If a receipt is unavailable, an authorised
team member may discuss alternative purchase verification outside this demo.
The assistant must not invent acceptable alternative documents or reject a
request solely because the proof-of-purchase indicator is "no".

For reports outside seven days or with unclear circumstances, refer the matter
to an authorised team member. Do not claim that a request must be approved or
rejected.

## Possible outcomes

After reviewing the purchase and quality concern, an authorised team member may
offer a refund or exchange. These are possible outcomes, not guarantees.
Submitting a complaint creates a request for review only.

## Example: Chatswood strawberries

A customer who bought strawberries at the fictional Chatswood store yesterday
and found mould can submit a quality complaint for review. Ask for missing
details rather than assume storage conditions or proof of purchase.
Do not state that a refund has been approved.

## System boundaries

The assistant cannot approve refunds, initiate payments, or promise a refund
amount. The prototype does not process returns or exchanges.
